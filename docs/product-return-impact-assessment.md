# Product returns: impact assessment and proposed policy

Status: design for owner review; no posting or migration implemented. The existing application and its authorized Phase 8, 9, 11 and invoice-discount rules remain in force.

## Findings

`invoice_items` preserves paid and free quantities, unit price, promotion discount, allocated invoice discount and final line total. Product-only inventory does not provide a reliable product expiry date. The existing historical batch structures do not establish expiry for every product-only sale. The phrase “approximately two years” does not specify a start date, cutoff, or tolerance. Do not derive eligibility from an invented date.

`Invoices::outstanding`, invoice status, receipt posting and credit checks currently subtract only posted payment allocations from the invoice total. `Reports` uses the same assumption, and its void reconciliation requires cash paid plus cancelled debt to equal the invoice total. A return settlement cannot be inserted as a payment without overstating collections, and cannot be treated as an invoice discount without misrepresenting the approved discount snapshots. This requires an explicit change to financial semantics.

## Proposed financial treatment for approval

1. Keep the original sale and the new invoice's gross, promotion discounts, invoice discount and payable snapshots unchanged. Record the return as a separate immutable linked transaction, with returned paid/free quantities, original line references, valuation, actor, reason, physical receipt confirmation and non-saleable disposition. Never create a saleable stock movement for returned goods.
2. Record a unique, immutable settlement against the new invoice: applied = min(approved return value, new invoice payable), forfeited = approved minus applied. The adjustment is neither a cash payment nor a promotion. Cash applied is limited to payable minus adjustment; subsequent payments and credit checks use the remaining receivable. No credit or refund is created from forfeited value.
3. Derive active outstanding as invoice payable less return settlement less actual posted cash allocations. On a void, restore only new invoice stock using the existing reversal, cancel remaining debt, retain the original return and settlement, and create refund due only for actual posted cash. Add an immutable administrator-review flag for the voided return; do not reuse the settlement. Reconciliation must account for settlement separately.
4. Report new invoice sales and return values separately. Proposed company net sales for a period: issued invoice net sales less approved return value recorded in that period; show applied and forfeited portions separately, with clear handling of later voids. Collections remain actual posted cash. Product-level and filtered reporting must attribute return values to original lines, avoiding double-counting the settlement as a discount or cash. The exact treatment of forfeiture and voided linked invoices in net revenue needs owner confirmation before implementation.
5. To value partial quantities deterministically, allocate each original line's final payable cents across its paid units: quotient cents per unit, with the remainder assigned to the first units. Consume unit positions sequentially across returns under the existing posting gate; free units have zero value. This makes all partial returns add exactly to the original line total and retains the already allocated invoice discount. Missing or inconsistent snapshots, voided original invoices, and unverifiable quantities are blocked. An original invoice with unpaid debt requires an explicit policy decision before allowing its products to fund a new invoice.

## Eligibility and authorization

Proposed confirmation: show original invoice date and an explicit declaration by the representative that the returned goods satisfy the owner's two-year policy, were physically received and are unsuitable for resale; require a reason and preserve the declaration. This is not an automatic two-year calculation. Owner confirmation of this eligibility method is needed. Representatives are authorized to finalize directly within current customer and invoice scope; no administrator approval is required for online returns. Offline finalization remains blocked. The request's separate reference to offline admin approval cannot enable an offline adjustment without a separately approved offline protocol.

## Execution and verification after approval

Before any migration, make and verify a fresh private backup of the exact application schema, without modifying unrelated databases or deploying. Use a new additive, checksum-verified migration and a disposable isolated test schema. Lock the existing shared inventory gate before validating return quantities, customer scope, receipt and checkout; commit return records, settlement, invoice, new-sale stock movements, actual cash and audit together. Preserve UUID/payload idempotency and revalidate the reviewed totals at posting. Update every balance, payment, void, document, report, CSV and reconciliation path, with Sinhala/English labels. Test rollback, competing requests, existing offline signatures and sale flows, and document any hardware testing limitations.
